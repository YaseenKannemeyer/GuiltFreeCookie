# Packages and pricing

What to charge clients for sites built the way Guilt Free Cookie was built, run as a **side hustle**: evenings and weekends, about **20 hours a week**, next to a day job. Prices checked **7 November 2026**, at **R18 to the US dollar**. Running costs per service are in [COSTS.md](COSTS.md).

## How the money works

Every client pays three separate things:

1. **The build**: a one-off fee, paid in milestones.
2. **Running costs**: Azure, Vercel, domain, Brevo and the rest. The client owns every account and their card is billed directly. You are added as an admin. You never carry their bills, and if they leave nothing has to move.
3. **Your monthly care fee**: your time and your margin. It covers keeping the site up, secure and improving.

PayFast fees and courier costs come out of each sale. They never pass through you.

### Signature Store

Everything built in this project, built again for the client's brand.

- **Discovery**: 2 interviews, an inspiration board, the client's sketches turned into wireframes, and our own ideas on top
- **Branding**: logo, colours, type, a brand guide (PDF)
- **3 design directions** to choose from, then 2 rounds of changes on the chosen one
- **Frontend**: a Three.js scene and GSAP scroll motion made for this brand, mobile first, fast on cheap phones
- **Backend**: products, sizes and pack options, cart, stock, PayFast payments, delivery with rules for free shipping, courier booking and PUDO lockers
- **Owner dashboard**: orders, stock, products, customers, refunds, shipping
- **Email**: order confirmations, shipping updates and the shop's own address, sent through Brevo
- **Monitoring**: error alerts (Sentry), uptime checks and a status page (Better Stack), a sales and health dashboard (Grafana)
- **Imagery**: up to 25 product images. The client's photos edited and improved, or new ones generated from the real product
- **Found on Google**: SEO, GEO (answers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI can quote), Search Console and indexing, Google Business Profile set up, a review request email after delivery
- **Launch**: staging and production, a test checklist, and a handover session on the dashboard

### Studio Store

The same shop, backend and dashboard as Signature, reused and styled for the client.

- 1 interview, inspiration board, **2 design directions**, 2 rounds of changes
- GSAP scroll motion and page transitions. No 3D scene made for the brand (can be added, see add-ons)
- Logo clean-up and a 1-page brand sheet (full brand guide is an add-on)
- Up to 12 product images
- SEO, Search Console, Google Business Profile set up
- Same email and monitoring as Signature

Lead with this one. Most food businesses can afford it, and because the backend and dashboard are already built, it pays you the most per hour.

### Brand Site

A site of 5–7 pages that sells the brand without a checkout: menu or catalogue, story, gallery, contact, and orders by WhatsApp or an enquiry form.

- 1 interview, inspiration board, **2 design directions**, 1 round of changes
- GSAP motion, built from scratch, no template
- Up to 8 images improved or generated
- SEO, Search Console, Google Business Profile set up
- Hosted on **Cloudflare Pages** (free, and its terms allow business use) or Vercel Pro ($20/month)

### Portfolio

One page, still designed from scratch.

- 1 interview, inspiration board, **1 design direction** plus 1 round of changes
- One signature animation (scroll story, hero reveal or similar)
- Contact form, SEO basics, Search Console
- Hosted on Cloudflare Pages: the client pays for the domain only

## How much you can take on

At 20 hours a week, run **one build at a time**: one Signature, or one Studio plus one Brand Site or Portfolio. Book the next client for when the current build ends instead of starting two big ones together. Keep about 5 hours a week free for care clients; that covers around 8–10 of them.

## What the numbers mean for you

A Signature build is about 300 hours over 15 weeks: discovery and brand 30, design 45, 3D and motion 85, backend and dashboard 60 (most of it reused from this project), payments, shipping and email 20, imagery 20, SEO 15, monitoring and launch 25. At R95,000 less R8,000 of tools, that's **≈ R290/hour**. The first-client price works out to **≈ R240/hour**.

| Package | Hours | Build | Per hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signature | ≈ 300 | R95,000 | ≈ R290 |
| Studio Store | ≈ 120 | R45,000 | ≈ R375 |
| Brand Site | ≈ 60 | R22,000 | ≈ R365 |
| Portfolio | ≈ 25 | R8,500 | ≈ R340 |

That's below agency rates on purpose. In return, you take the first 2 Signature clients for a case study, permission to show the work and a testimonial. Once Guilt Free Cookie and one client shop have real sales to show, raise Signature to R120,000–R150,000.

The care fees build up month by month. Two Signature clients, three Studio clients and three Brand Sites bring in **≈ R14,400 a month** for about 15 hours of included work.

## Before promising anything

- **Don't promise "first on Google".** Nobody controls rankings, and clients remember the promise. Promise the work instead: a fast, well-built site, Google Business Profile set up, Search Console, structured data, and reviews collected the right way. Showing up high for the brand's own name and in local "near me" searches is realistic. Ranking first for "cookies" is not.
- **Reviews: ask every customer, reward no one.** Google's rules ban paying or giving discounts for reviews, and ban only asking happy customers. Breaking either can get reviews removed or the profile suspended. The review request email goes to every customer after delivery.
- **AI product images have to show the real product.** Improve lighting, background and setting, but not the size, the look or what's in the pack. A misleading image is a consumer complaint waiting to happen. Keep the client's originals.
- **Vercel Hobby is not allowed for business sites.** Every store needs Vercel Pro ($20/month); it's included in the running costs above. Brand Sites and Portfolios use Cloudflare Pages to avoid that cost.
- **Put the scope in writing:** number of design directions, rounds of changes and images, response times, and the R350/hour rate for anything else. Most overruns on fixed-price builds come from extra rounds of changes, and on a side hustle those hours come out of your evenings.
- **Check your employment contract** for rules on outside work or moonlighting before taking the first client.

## Later: the owners dashboard

Once you have several clients, build a dashboard of your own (a small CRM) that lists every client with: package, care plan, hours used, billing status, each site's uptime and error count from Better Stack and Sentry, and links into their Azure, Vercel, PayFast and Brevo accounts. Until then a spreadsheet does the job. The care fees pay for building it.
